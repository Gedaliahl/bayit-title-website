L'Hermitage is a neighborhood of zero-lot-line homes on Parcels A, A-1 and A-2 of the Heron Bay Three plat (Plat Book 159, Page 41) in Coral Springs. WCI Communities Limited Partnership sold the whole tract to a builder by a warranty deed recorded 3 January 1996, with the Declaration of Neighborhood Covenants attached as Exhibit 1 (OR 24337/694, Instr 96004140). The covenants required the builder to form a Neighborhood Association before the first home sale (Declaration Art. II ¶ 4, p. 5), and a 1998 amendment names it: "L'Hermitage at Heron Bay Homeowners' Association, Inc." (OR 28495/665, p. 1).

**We do not hold L'Hermitage's own declaration, articles or by-laws.** Those are the documents that would govern maintenance, assessments, leasing and use home by home. This page covers only the 1996 covenants and the 1998 amendment, the newer recorded 2 July 1998, and it does not guess at what the missing documents say. "Declaration" below means the covenants attached to the deed, cited by PDF page. The [Heron Bay master page](/communities/heron-bay) applies to every lot. Nothing here replaces the association's estoppel certificate.

## What does a closing here need beyond the master's?

- **Two estoppels**, from L'Hermitage at Heron Bay Homeowners' Association, Inc. and from the master. Confirm first that L'Hermitage is an active corporation and who manages it.
- **L'Hermitage's own declaration, articles and by-laws.** Without them we cannot say who maintains and insures the roofs, walls and yards of these homes, or whether a sale or lease needs approval, or a transfer fee or right of first refusal applies.
- **A search that finds covenants filed as a deed exhibit.** They are recorded inside a warranty deed (OR 24337/694–708), not under a declaration caption, so a search that relies on document type may miss them and the 1998 amendment. [The Pointe](/communities/heron-bay/the-pointe), on Heron Bay Four, carries covenants recorded the same way.
- **No resale fee in the documents we hold.** The covenants' $500-per-home master Capital Payment was owed by the builder, by a deadline the 1998 amendment moved to 31 December 1999 (Art. III ¶ 2, p. 9; OR 28495/665, p. 1). The master estoppel shows whether anything is still claimed against a lot.
- **No master Neighborhood Expenses.** The covenants say this "is not a Benefitted Neighborhood" under the master declaration (Art. I ¶ 5, p. 3).

## Can a home here be leased?

The documents we hold say nothing about leasing. The master's rules apply: one lease in any 12 months, with tenants registered before they move in ([master leasing rules](/communities/heron-bay#can-a-heron-bay-home-be-leased)). L'Hermitage's own declaration may add limits of its own; until we have read it, we cannot say whether it does.

## What title points come up here?

- **An Educational Impact Lien Agreement.** The deed conveyed the land "FURTHER SUBJECT TO: Educational Impact Lien Agreement, dated December 4, 1995, and recorded in Official Records Book 24213, at Page 65 … which the Grantee shall assume and agree to pay" (OR 24337/694, p. 1). The agreement is not in our set. It should be pulled to confirm it has been satisfied or released.
- **The 30-year term has run.** The covenants bind the neighborhood "for a period of thirty (30) years from the date this DECLARATION is recorded, after which time this DECLARATION shall be extended for successive periods of ten (10) years". After the first 30 years, owners of two-thirds of the Property Units may terminate or modify them by a recorded instrument (Art. III ¶ 13, pp. 13–14). The first 30 years ended on **3 January 2026**.
- **No preservation notice.** Covenants that predate a lot's root of title are extinguished unless an exception applies ([Fla. Stat. § 712.04](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0700-0799/0712/Sections/0712.04.html)); an association preserves them by recording a notice ([§ 712.05](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0700-0799/0712/Sections/0712.05.html)). No notice for these covenants is in our set, and the master's 2024 notice does not list them. Whether they still bind a particular lot turns on its chain of title, a question for underwriting.
- **The Buffer Parcels.** The 1998 amendment says Parcels A-1 and A-2 "shall hereafter be owned and maintained by the Association", meaning the master, and ends the builder's and L'Hermitage's duty to maintain them (OR 28495/665, p. 1). The master did not join the amendment, and no deed of those parcels is in our set. Current ownership should be confirmed from the property appraiser.
- **The developer kept broad powers.** It alone may amend the covenants, for minor changes only (Art. III ¶ 12, p. 13); it resolves any conflict with the master declaration, "and its decision shall be final" (¶ 11); and it may file a claim of lien against a site for the cost of curing an owner's maintenance failure (¶ 6.B, p. 13). The search should look for any such claim.
- **Also cited but not in our set:** the Heron Bay Three plat (Plat Book 159, Page 41) and the power of attorney under which the 1998 amendment was signed (OR 26908/845).

## Common questions

### Who maintains the roof and walls of a L'Hermitage home?

We cannot say from the documents we hold. The covenants put upkeep of each site and home on the "OWNER or Neighborhood Association" (Art. III ¶ 6.A, p. 12) without dividing it; L'Hermitage's own declaration would.

### Does the buyer pay L'Hermitage anything at closing?

Not known. Nothing in the documents we hold charges a resale fee, but L'Hermitage's own documents may, and its estoppel will show what it claims.

### Can I run a business from home?

No: "no business may be conducted on any part thereof, nor shall any building or ZERO LOT LINE DWELLING, or any portion thereof, be used or maintained as a professional office" (Art. II ¶ 1, p. 4).

### What are the building rules?

At least 2,000 square feet of living area (Art. II ¶ 3, p. 5); no higher than 35 feet, or 22 feet along the south 196.73 feet of the west perimeter (¶ 2.D, p. 5); cement or clay tile or slate roofs, never cedar or asphalt shingle (¶ 7.A, pp. 6–7).

### What about garages and driveways?

A garage for at least two cars, no carports, and vehicle repairs only inside the garage (Art. II ¶ 5.A–B, p. 6). Driveways must be stamped concrete, pavers or brick; plain concrete, asphalt and loose stone are not allowed (¶ 8.A, p. 7).

### What about pets, fences and signs?

The covenants we hold have no pet, fence or sign rules for the homes, so the master's rules apply ([master page](/communities/heron-bay)). L'Hermitage's own declaration may add more.

### Have the covenants ended now that 30 years have passed?

The covenants themselves say they extend for successive ten-year periods, and that at any time after the first 30 years owners of two-thirds of the Property Units may terminate or modify them by a recorded instrument (Art. III ¶ 13, pp. 13–14). Whether they survive for a particular lot under Chapter 712 is a question for underwriting.
