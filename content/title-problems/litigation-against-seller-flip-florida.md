## What actually connects a lawsuit to the property?

A suit against a person is not automatically a cloud on title. Something has to tie it to the parcel. The usual mechanism is a recorded lis pendens — a notice recorded in the official records that litigation is pending which could affect title to the property.

Where nothing has been recorded, a pending lawsuit may not surface in a title search at all. That is worth stating plainly, because a search is a search of the records, not an investigation of the seller.

## Why does a short ownership period matter?

Not because a quick resale is improper — it is not. It matters because there has been less time for the record to settle, and because the examination has to look closely at how the seller acquired.

## When should an attorney be involved?

Whenever litigation touches the property. A buyer purchasing from a seller in active litigation needs their own Florida real estate attorney advising them on what they are taking on — and where a lis pendens is recorded, that advice is not optional.
