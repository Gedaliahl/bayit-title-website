# The questions only First American or a lawyer can answer

Seven of the nine drafts cannot go to `status: reviewed` no matter what the team
answers about its own timelines, costs and practice, because each one carries at
least one fact that is an **underwriting position** or a **legal conclusion**.
Those two pages that are *not* on this list —
`hoa-approval-delay-closing-florida` and
`non-standard-purchase-contract-florida-closing` — are blocked only on Category C
(what Bayit Title knows) and publish without anybody outside the office.

The questions below are written to be sent as-is. Flag IDs match the
`pending_confirmation` lists in the content front-matter, so an answer can be
walked straight back to the page it unblocks.

## For the First American agency representative

Ask for these **in writing** — a bulletin or an email we can keep in the file.
An underwriting position relayed from memory is the thing this list exists to
avoid, and the project brief only permits First American material where the
bulletin is supplied.

**FA-1 — Remote online notarization, generally.** `SG-05`
Does First American accept instruments notarized by a Florida online notary
under Fla. Stat. § 117.209 in closings we insure? Which document types, if any,
are excluded — are there instruments on which you require wet ink regardless of
what the statute permits? Do you require the RON platform to be on an approved
list, and is there one?

**FA-2 — RON where the signer is outside the United States.** `FS-05`
Where the online notary is physically in Florida and the signer is abroad, do
you accept the notarial act without additional requirements? Are there countries
you will not accept a remote signing from? Do you require identity proofing or
credential analysis beyond the statutory minimum when the signer presents a
foreign passport under § 117.201(6)?

**FA-3 — Foreign acknowledgments and the apostille.**
For a deed acknowledged abroad in one of the forms § 695.03(3) allows — a
foreign notary with an official seal, a civil-law notary, a commissioner of
deeds, a US consular officer — do you require an apostille or a chain of
authentication *in addition* to the statutory form? Does the answer vary by
country, and is there a list? Where a civil-law notary produces an instrument in
its own form rather than a Florida-style certificate, what do you need to see?

**FA-4 — Open permits and code enforcement.** `OP-06`
What is your coverage position on an open or expired building permit, and on a
code enforcement violation that has not been recorded? Is any endorsement
available that reaches either, and on what conditions? Do you require a
municipal lien search — on every insured purchase, or only in stated
circumstances? Where an open permit is known and unresolved at closing, what do
you require: an escrow holdback, an indemnity, an exception on Schedule B-II?

**FA-5 — Access.** `AC-03`
What is the standard access exception language in commitments issued on your
forms? Is an access endorsement available where the public records do not
establish legal access to the parcel? What facts do you need in order to
consider one, and what are the circumstances in which you will decline? Where a
recorded easement is being obtained to cure, do you review the instrument and
legal description before closing?

**FA-6 — Identity affidavit for a name-match judgment.** `JG-05`
What form of affidavit do you accept to establish that a judgment debtor of the
same or a similar name is not our seller? Do you have a preferred or required
form we should be using? What supporting documentation do you require alongside
it — a search on the debtor's identifiers, a credit report, a written statement
from the judgment creditor's counsel — and does that change with the size of the
judgment or the closeness of the name match?

**FA-7 — A judgment not being paid because the property is homestead.**
Where a recorded judgment is not satisfied at closing on the basis that the
property is the seller's homestead under Fla. Const. art. X, § 4, what do you
require in order to close: a specific affidavit, evidence of continuous
residency, a survey showing the acreage limit? Will you insure without exception
on that basis, or do you take exception to the judgment?

**FA-8 — Pending litigation and a recorded lis pendens.** `LT-08`
What is your position where the seller is a defendant in active litigation? Does
it differ according to what the suit seeks — money only, versus an interest in
the property (specific performance, quiet title, rescission, constructive trust,
partition)? Where a lis pendens is recorded, what will clear the requirement: a
voluntary discharge, an order discharging it, a bond under § 48.23, a release? Is
there any circumstance in which you would insure over a recorded lis pendens,
and on what terms? Where litigation is pending but nothing is recorded, do you
require anything at all?

**FA-9 — A seller who acquired recently.** `LT-04`
Do you require anything additional where the seller acquired the property within
the last twelve or twenty-four months — a search beyond the ordinary period,
review of the prior owner's conveyance, scrutiny of a preceding distressed or
tax-deed transfer, a specific affidavit? Is there a look-back period at which
your requirements change?

**FA-10 — Items required from a bankruptcy sale.** `BK-03`
What do you require before insuring a purchase from a bankruptcy estate: the
motion, the notice and proof of service, a docket printout, the entered order,
evidence of the trustee's appointment and authority, a finding of good faith
under 11 U.S.C. § 363(m)? Where the order waives the 14-day stay under Fed. R.
Bankr. P. 6004(h), do you close on entry, or do you still require the appeal
period to run? Where a sale is free and clear under § 363(f), do you rely on the
order alone to omit the liens from Schedule B, or do you require separate
releases?

**FA-11 — A closed prior bankruptcy in the chain.** `BK-05`
Where a prior owner's bankruptcy appears in the chain of title and the case has
been closed, what documentation clears it: the trustee's deed with the order,
an order of abandonment, the discharge, evidence the property was scheduled and
administered? Where the paper does not resolve it, when will you insure over the
question rather than require the case be reopened?

## For counsel

**L-1 — Tenancy by the entireties.** `judgment` page, still flagged `[VERIFY]`
For a Florida real estate attorney. Under current Florida law, does a money
judgment against one spouse attach to real property held by both spouses as
tenants by the entireties? What creates the entireties presumption where a
married couple takes title, and what rebuts it? At a closing where only one
spouse is the judgment debtor, what has to be established and documented for the
judgment to be treated as not attaching? **May we state on a public page that
such a judgment does not attach, and with what qualification?** This one turns on
case law rather than a statute, which is why it was not drafted.

**L-2 — The FAR/BAR contract and open permits.** `OP-04`
In the current FAR/BAR Residential Contract for Sale and Purchase and the "AS IS"
version, which paragraph allocates responsibility for an open or expired permit
and for unpermitted work, and what does it require of the seller and by when?
Separately: the form is licensed rather than public — may we quote it, or should
the page paraphrase without quoting?

**L-3 — FIRPTA and the settlement agent.** `foreign-seller` page
For tax counsel or a CPA. The withholding obligation under 26 U.S.C. § 1445 sits
with the buyer as withholding agent. What is the settlement agent's role and
exposure where we remit on the buyer's behalf, and how should the page describe
what we do and do not take responsibility for? Is our description of the
$300,000 and $1,000,000 thresholds, and of Forms 8288, 8288-A and 8288-B, safe to
publish as written?

**L-4 — The bankruptcy page, as a whole.** `buying-property-bankruptcy-estate-florida`
For bankruptcy counsel. This page describes the 14-day stay under FRBP 6004(h),
the 14-day appeal window under FRBP 8002(a)(1), the good-faith purchaser
protection under § 363(m), and the scope of a free-and-clear provision. Is each
accurate as stated, and is a public-facing page from a title agency the right
place to state any of it? Flag anything that should come out rather than be
corrected.
